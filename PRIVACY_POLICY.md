# Privacy Policy — OpenHood

Last updated: August 4, 2026

This policy explains how OpenHood handles information when you use the app.

## Information we collect

OpenHood collects only what you choose to enter directly into the app: your vehicle's details (make, model, year, and related configuration), any incidents or symptoms you describe through "Something Happened," and any notes or maintenance information you add. OpenHood does not require an account, an email address, or a name to use the app.

## How this information is used and stored

Everything you enter is stored locally on your device only. OpenHood does not operate a server, does not send your vehicle or incident data anywhere, and does not have a backend database. Your information is used solely to run the features you're interacting with in the moment — for example, showing your saved vehicles or generating guidance based on what you've described.

We do not sell your information. We do not use it for advertising. We do not track you across other companies' apps or websites, because OpenHood has no mechanism to do so.

## Location

OpenHood does not request or collect your location itself. If you use the "find a shop" feature, OpenHood hands your search off to Apple Maps, which may separately ask your permission to use your location under its own privacy practices — that request and any resulting data belongs to Maps, not OpenHood.

## Camera

If you use the VIN scanner during vehicle setup, OpenHood accesses your device's camera to read the VIN. The camera image is used only to complete that scan and is not stored or transmitted afterward.

## Third parties

OpenHood does not share your information with any third-party service, analytics provider, or advertising network, because it does not use any. ## Retention and deletion

Your information stays on your device for as long as you keep the app installed, or until you delete it yourself. You can permanently erase all your data at any time from Settings. Deleting the app from your device also removes all locally stored data along with it.

## Your choices

You control everything OpenHood stores, directly in the app — edit or remove a vehicle, delete an incident, or erase everything at once from Settings. There is no account to manage and nothing stored outside your device to separately request access to or deletion of.

We may update this policy if OpenHood's practices change — for example, if a future version adds features that require a server or an account. Any such change would be reflected here with an updated date.
